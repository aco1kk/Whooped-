Live heart rate

Pairs with any Bluetooth device that broadcasts heart rate: WHOOP (turn on heart rate broadcast in the WHOOP app first), Polar, Garmin, Wahoo, Coros and most chest straps. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone. iPhone browsers don't support Bluetooth.

Import your history

      
Upload your WHOOP data export: the .zip file, or the CSVs inside it. Request it from the WHOOP app's settings and it arrives by email. Any other CSV works too if it has a date column and columns like sleep, HRV, resting heart rate or steps.
